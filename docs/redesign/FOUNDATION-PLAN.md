# Expanded WiSim foundation objective - 13 November 2026

The current execution order is recorded in [ORDERED-PLAN.md](ORDERED-PLAN.md), dated 17 November 2026. It adds the optional home/compartment editor and supersedes the earlier priorities below.

This incorporates the user's latest requirements and preserves the unfinished electrical/sandbox/designer objective. Detailed manufacturer device artwork follows the working foundation. Source-backed real products and useful rating comparisons are part of that foundation.

The user cancelled the prior tracked goal and explicitly started a rewritten goal. The new tracked objective is active. Desktop mouse/keyboard behaviour, working live calculations, designer and 3D functionality take priority over touch/small-screen refinements. The desktop UI review includes terminal-detail component switching, reuse of available full 3D geometry and more refined schematic arrangement. Existing mobile checks remain regression evidence, not the primary development target.

## Required outcomes

1. Continue independent electrical checks, live readings and large-system verification. Mistakes remain possible in the sandbox, with located severity-coloured warnings, a complete diagnostics panel and optional one-at-a-time fixes. Designer output must pass the supported design constraints and clearly identify incomplete verification.
2. All settings and positioning controls must be reachable at normal browser zoom, including short desktop windows and 3D. Panels need usable scrolling, sufficient control sizes, sensible collapse behaviour and hover explanations.
3. Wheel zooms the schematic. Ctrl-wheel pans vertically; Shift-wheel pans horizontally. Rectangle selection and Ctrl-click select multiple items. Group movement commits once and preserves wiring. Multi-selection offers deletion, keeping only the selection, a separate new system, copying/pasting to another system and download. Destructive actions explain lost equipment/connections and support cancellation and Undo.
4. Organisation has layered, compact and screen-shaped choices. It changes diagram positions without changing electrical connections or physical cable lengths. Optimising electrical equipment is a separate operation with explicit tradeoffs.
5. PV arrays and battery banks contain real individual units/branches but can display compactly. They must expand/split, accept additional units and expose clear counts, series/parallel topology and warnings. Saved data, live math, product matching, schedules and drawings must agree with the underlying units.
6. Catalogue comparisons always offer the nearest sufficiently documented alternatives when any exist, including differences in voltage, power, capacity, current and surge. Applying a product stays possible in the sandbox. Multiple-unit alternatives must create real equipment and wiring with explicit topology; they must not simply multiply a displayed rating or assume batteries support arbitrary series wiring.
7. Designer evaluates alternative module sizes and counts, multiple MPPTs, voltage/string/current/cable constraints and individual battery packs. It needs explicit quantities, terminal schedules and stable drawing references. Complete termination hardware, regional ampacity/protection/earthing and multi-phase/schedule/yield validation remain required.
8. Physical auto-routing requires at least one wall. Routes must follow valid wall/roof/support surfaces and respect device terminal approaches, supported transitions and bend directions. Unsupported spans and impossible paths must be explained rather than silently hanging cable in space. Angled roofs require routing in their own surface coordinates; global-axis-only bends are insufficient. Route length feeds the electrical calculation, with preview/apply/cancel/Undo and persistence.
9. Verify useful multimeter modes and add instruments where the supported electrical model can provide honest readings. Resistance/continuity must handle energised circuits explicitly. Do not invent readings unsupported by the solver.
10. Terminal detail stays a useful working view: switch directly between board components, follow connected equipment, inspect individual wires without losing the current component, use the same available 3D geometry as the workbench, orbit/frame it with the mouse and attach probes to visible terminals. Procedural dimensional approximations must remain labelled until sourced full assets exist.
11. Expand sourced popular-brand equipment and rating coverage, with source/license records and explicit verification status. Correct product ratings, dimensions and connection information take precedence over detailed artwork. Accurate models follow verified controls, routing, math, diagnostics and designer behaviour.

## Next implementation gates

Implemented compact group metadata preserves every underlying device/wire and map visible ports back to real terminal IDs. Expand/ungroup is a view/topology-metadata operation, not destruction of the circuit. Translated diagram groups must move their member diagram coordinates together and undo once. Saved versions, copying, product replacement and exports must preserve valid group membership. Export quantities remain individual units.

Implemented group source-power totals are derived from the solved network; richer boundary/net summaries remain open. Sum individual source powers where appropriate, but do not sum current through panels in series; use boundary currents or one current per string with an explicit sign convention. Internal cable losses must not disappear from the presentation. Physical models remain individual equipment even when the schematic is compact.

Multiple-controller design must partition actual strings among independent controllers and retain each controller's voltage/current/power limits and output branch/protection. Alternative panel selection must evaluate sourced per-module ratings and temperature/string constraints rather than changing only the displayed wattage/count. Surface routing must record support relationships so moving a wall/roof does not leave stale floating bends. Impossible/unsupported routes remain explicit failures, not diagonal fallbacks.
